English has no grammatical gender and minimal noun declension (only singular vs. plural, e.g. cat/cats, and the possessive 's). If the word is a noun or adjective, produce one sentence for the singular form and one for the plural form (skip the plural if the word is uncountable or invariant); if it is a personal or possessive pronoun, produce one sentence per grammatical person (I, you, he/she/it, we, they) using the correct pronoun form for that person. If the word is a verb, produce one sentence for EACH of: base form (I/you/we/they), 3rd-person singular present (-s form: he/she/it), past simple, past participle (has/have + form), and the -ing form (progressive) — five sentences minimum, correctly formed, do not stop early or skip any.
