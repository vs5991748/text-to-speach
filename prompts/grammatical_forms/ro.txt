Romanian nouns, pronouns, and adjectives have 3 genders (masculine, feminine, neuter) and 2 numbers (singular, plural); adjectives agree in gender and number with the noun. If the word is a noun, pronoun, or adjective, produce one sentence for EACH gender the word can take (skip genders it cannot take) combined with EACH number, correctly declined and agreeing in gender/number, including the definite-article suffix form (e.g. -ul/-a/-le for definite singular) where natural. If the word is a verb, produce one sentence for EACH of the six grammatical persons (eu, tu, el/ea, noi, voi, ei/ele) in the indicative present, PLUS one sentence in the subjunctive and one in the imperative (2nd person) — eight sentences minimum, correctly conjugated, do not stop early or skip any.
