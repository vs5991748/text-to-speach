Portuguese nouns, pronouns, and adjectives have 2 genders (masculine, feminine) and 2 numbers (singular, plural); adjectives agree in gender and number with the noun. If the word is a noun, pronoun, or adjective, produce one sentence for EACH gender the word can take (skip the other gender if inapplicable) combined with EACH number, correctly agreeing — minimum two sentences, four if both genders apply. If the word is a verb, produce one sentence for EACH of the six grammatical persons (eu, tu/você, ele/ela, nós, vós/vocês, eles/elas) in the present indicative, PLUS one sentence in the imperative — seven sentences minimum, correctly conjugated, do not stop early or skip any.
